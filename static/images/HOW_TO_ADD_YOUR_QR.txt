  HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN UPI QR CODE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Step 1: Open PhonePe / GPay / Paytm
Step 2: Go to "Receive Money" or "My QR Code"  
Step 3: Save / Screenshot your QR code image
Step 4: Rename it to:  upi_qr.png
Step 5: Copy it to:    static/images/upi_qr.png
         (this folder - right here!)

That's it! Your QR will show automatically on
the payment page when UPI is selected.
